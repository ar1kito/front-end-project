about

printmaking expertise

Leek Chenz is a printmaker known for their exceptional work in woodblock printing, etching, and screen printing. With a keen eye for detail and a deep understanding of various printing techniques, Leek Chenz's artistry reflects themes of nature, culture, and the human experience.

What I do

Explore my range of printmaking services designed to bring your artistic visions to life. From woodblock printing to screen printing, each piece is crafted with precision and care.

woodblock printing

traditional craftsmanship

Experience the art of woodblock printing with my meticulously hand-carved designs that capture intricate details and timeless beauty.

Etching

Artistic Impressions

Discover the art of etching, where rich textures and deep impressions come together to create visually stunning prints that evoke emotion and depth.

Screen Printing

Modern Expression

Delve into the world of screen printing and witness the vibrant tapestry of urban life come alive in each print, showcasing energy and diversity.
